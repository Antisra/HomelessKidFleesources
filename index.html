Youth Shelter & Housing Resources
Ages 16–24

Need immediate help? Call or text 1-800-RUNAWAY (1-800-786-2929) — 24/7, free, confidential.

About this resource

Unfortunately, due to ageism, adultism, and ageist laws, there still aren't really youth shelters in the U.S., and those that used to exist during documentaries like "Children of the Night" and LA street kid documentaries have been shut down. People feel kids should just go "back home," and other youth shelters are for foster kids or foster homeless shelter placements for the kids they don't throw on the streets or in a hotel when they have no placement, or are too old for people to want to foster.

Many youth shelters want ID, which is harder to get for legal and other reasons if you're young, and they can't harbor runaways or missing people knowingly -- which is bad because many kicked-out people, runaways, and abuse escapees are reported missing as a smear campaign or blackmail.

For unaccompanied homeless youth who have nowhere to go and are trying to run as encampment sweeps and imprisonment get worse -- even for those who legally can't go to shelters and are too young for the "help" that doesn't exist, and for those that were told that there's help out there but they have to hold onto dear life until 18, just to find out "help" still doesn't exist and they're now too old or still too young for everything, and will still be discriminated against for having no parents.
